Privacy Policy

TypeBridge

Effective Date: 23 July 2026

Introduction

Thank you for using TypeBridge. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information our application collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

Information We Collect

TypeBridge is designed to work directly between your Android device and your Windows computer over Bluetooth.

We do NOT collect:

  • Personal information
  • Email addresses
  • Passwords
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Videos
  • Location information
  • Messages
  • Financial information
  • Any information stored on our servers

Bluetooth Permission

TypeBridge requires Bluetooth permissions to establish a secure connection between your Android device and your Windows PC. Bluetooth is only used for communication between your own devices. No Bluetooth information is transmitted to our servers.

Keyboard and Touchpad Functions

The app sends keyboard and touchpad input directly to your connected computer. These inputs remain between your phone and your computer and are never collected, stored, monitored, or transmitted to any external server.

Data Storage

TypeBridge stores only local application preferences on your own device, such as:

  • Selected theme
  • Touchpad sensitivity
  • Interface preferences
  • Other local settings

These settings never leave your device.

Internet Connection

TypeBridge does not require an internet connection for its core Bluetooth functionality. If future versions introduce cloud features or online services, this Privacy Policy will be updated.

Third-Party Services

At this time, TypeBridge does not use:

  • Advertising SDKs
  • Analytics services
  • Tracking technologies
  • Cloud databases
  • User accounts

Children's Privacy

TypeBridge does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Security

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Communication occurs directly between your Android device and your Windows PC over Bluetooth without routing your data through our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any future changes will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
